# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

This project discusses specifications, staleness, and deterministic verdicts.
Debate the artifact, never the person. Concretely:

- Critique the diff, the schema, or the verdict — with evidence from the
  repository, not speculation about motives.
- Assume the reporter of a bug ran the command they say they ran.
- Keep generated output, logs, and links safe to open in public.
- No harassment, insults, spam, or doxxing. Ever.

## Scope

This standard applies to the repository (issues, pull requests, reviews), the
project website, and any project-run chat or meeting space.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, lock, or remove content and may ban accounts that
repeatedly violate this standard. For a private report, open a
[GitHub security advisory or contact the organization](https://github.com/orgs/vanguard-lint)
rather than escalating in public. Reports are handled quietly; we do not
discuss reporter identities.

Repeated or severe violations in a contribution of code follow the same path:
the contribution is rejected first, then the account is restricted.
